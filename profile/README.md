# Rebase

Bug reports that show what broke. From your team and customers.

Your customers don't have to show you what went wrong. Rebase keeps up to 24 hours of their recent visits in their own browser, so when something breaks they share it from one link: no screen-share, no reproducing it, no HAR file. Nothing leaves their browser until they choose.

Customers and teammates can also report a problem from the page itself. Each report arrives with the pages, clicks, failed requests and console errors that led up to it, plus a screenshot and an optional replay of the last minute. Reports go to Jira, Linear, GitHub, Asana or Slack.

Coding agents can read reports too. [mcp.rebase.dev](https://github.com/rebasedotdev/mcp.rebase.dev) is our MIT-licensed MCP server for Claude Code, Cursor and other MCP clients.
